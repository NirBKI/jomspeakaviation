The Story Behind CCAE

Bridging the Gap in Operational Fluency

Aviation English is more than passing a standardized test; it is the frontline barrier of aviation safety. However, many Malaysian aviation professionals—student pilots, cadet controllers, active aircrew—rarely use English outside duty hours, eroding confidence ahead of crucial English Language Proficiency (ELP) assessments.

ClearComms Aviation English (CCAE) is an independent, non-commercial initiative founded by Munir Mohamad. Having spent over 15 years in high-density radar and tower environments, Munir provides real-world, stress-free coaching designed by an active assessor who understands exact evaluation benchmarks.

ICAO Holistic Descriptors

Comprehensive Coaching Dimensions

Our 1-on-1 sessions diagnose strengths and resolve weaknesses across the core parameters evaluated during official ELP testing.

01

Pronunciation & Accent

Clear, intelligible phrasing with natural rhythm, stress, and intonation without regional interference impeding radiotelephony.

02

Grammatical Structure

Mastering complex and basic grammatical patterns consistently in operational contexts, reducing ambiguity under stress.

03

Aviation Vocabulary

Precise terminology covering technical systems, meteorological phenomena, emergency configurations, and passenger situations.

04

Fluency & Response Rate

Producing spontaneous, continuous speech at an operational tempo without inappropriate hesitation or speech blocks.

05

Operational Comprehension

Accurate understanding of international accents, subtle clearances, complex reroutings, and distorted audio signals.

06

Interactive Plain English

Immediate, appropriate responses during non-standard situations where standard phraseology is insufficient.
